# Security policy

Found a vulnerability in something Debb Labs builds or runs — one of our apps, websites or APIs? Please
tell us privately, and do not open a public issue.

- **Email** [info@debblabs.com](mailto:info@debblabs.com) with **"Security"** in the subject.
- Say what is affected (the app, site or API, and its version if you know it), how to reproduce the
  problem, and what an attacker could do with it.

We will acknowledge your report, keep you updated until it is fixed, and credit you if you would like to
be named. Please give us a reasonable time to fix it before disclosing it.
